Campus Press

The visual world is an Indian college notice board: xeroxed fest posters, stamped forms, taped-on stickers, hand-ruled section headers. Ink on warm paper, one loud orange, hard edges. It is deliberately not the cool-grey rounded-card look every template ships with — that look is why the app reads as generic.

01 — Ink & paper

The single highest-leverage change: surfaces move from cool slate (#F8FAFC) to warm paper (#F5F2EC), and body text from blue-grey to warm ink. Same layouts, completely different temperature.

03 — Type
Space Grotesk

Display / headings / all numerals. 700 only, tracking −1.2px at display size. Its slightly mechanical letterforms are what stop the app looking like every Inter-only product.

Inter

Body, 15px / 1.55. Sentence case. The shipped app sets body copy in uppercase font-black at 11px — that is the readability bug this fixes.

04 — The six signature devices

These carry the identity. Used consistently, they are recognisable in a screenshot with the logo cropped out.

1 · Section rule
Feed128 new

Eyebrow, 2px hard rule to the edge, tabular count on the right. Replaces the floating "See all" row. Reads as a ruled notice board.

2 · Sticker chip
Live now3 seats leftAlumni

2px border, 2px hard shadow, rotated 1.6°. For status and identity only — never for navigation.

3 · Hard stamp shadow

4px 4px 0 ink. Exactly one card per screen gets it — the one action that screen exists for. Everything else is a 1px hairline.

4 · Number stamp
41min#12campus

Dashed circle, tabular numerals. Streaks, ranks, scores, countdowns — the numbers students screenshot.

5 · Ticker
412 onlineFest week

Ink strip under the status bar on Home only. Campus is a live place; the app should say so before you scroll.

6 · Cut corner

18px notch on the top-right of secondary feature cards. A quiet repeat that ties unrelated screens together.

05 — Components
Buttons — 48px min
RegisterConfirmLaterSkip

One primary per screen. Primary = brand fill + 2px rule + ink text (contrast holds on orange). The stamp shadow belongs to the hero card, so a primary button inside a card or a docked bar drops it — otherwise the card clips it and the screen carries two stamps.

Role rings
NOAKHR

Student / alumni / recruiter. Never colour-only: a text sticker accompanies the ring wherever identity changes what the content means.

Chips
AllCampus
₹55k6 months

Rounded — the one soft shape in the system, so filters never read as stickers. Two sizes, and the size states intent: .chip is a 44px tap target for filters; .chip.tag is 28px, read-only metadata, and wraps in a .tagrow instead of scrolling.

Icons

Single 1.9px stroke weight, 22px default, 44×44 tap area. Outline only — no mixed fill/outline in one row. No emoji as icons, ever.

07 — Padding & the gutter

Every screen is audited against these in a headless pass; a violation is a build failure, not a judgement call. The list exists because each line here was a real defect found in the first cut of these mockups.

RuleWhat it prevents
One gutter: 20px, .padThe status bar, app bar, tabs, section rules and cards all start on the same vertical line. The status bar was at 22px and read as a wobble.
Card body padding is 16px, .pad-in.pad-in works standalone, not only as .card.pad-in. When it did not, the body under a media block got zero padding and every number stamp was clipped at the card edge.
An edge icon button overhangs by 11pxA 44px touch box aligned at 20px puts its glyph at 31px. The glyph lines up with the gutter; the box hangs off it.
A tile's box fills its columnA fixed 54px box centred in an 87px column sat 17px inside the gutter, so the icon grid never lined up with the rule above it.
Nothing with a hard shadow or a rotation sits flush against a clipping edge.card is overflow:hidden for its media. A stamped button or a rotated sticker at the padding edge gets sliced.
Horizontal scrollers bleed to the edge of the box that holds themA chiprow cut at an arbitrary inner point reads as a bug. Inside a card, metadata should not scroll at all — wrap it in a .tagrow.
.row never strips a card's own paddingA card that is a row keeps .pad-in; only nested rows go flush.
A docked bottom bar clears the home indicator.bottombar pads 20px + 10px at the bottom. A composer flush to the glass is unreachable on a notched device.
Number stamp captions fit inside the ringThe ring is 66px; the caption is capped at 56px and ellipsised. ELIGIBLE and CAMPUS used to spill past the dashes.
