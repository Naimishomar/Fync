Every screen here is built from what client/components/** actually renders — the fields, tabs, states and labels that exist today, and nothing that doesn't. The audit behind it is FUNCTIONALITY.md; tokens and components are in the design system sheet.

Rule for this folder: if a control is not in FUNCTIONALITY.md, it does not go in a screen. That is why there is no Google sign-in, no third home tab, and why the Utility Hub has three tools rather than twelve.
